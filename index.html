FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before placing an order.

How accurate is the lead data provided?
Our lead lists are verified using real-time validation tools (NeverBounce, ZeroBounce, Clay). We guarantee a bounce rate under 5% and replace invalid leads free of charge.
Do I need an Apollo account to order Apollo leads?
No. You don’t need an Apollo account or any browser extension. Share your target filters (industry, job title, company size and location) on WhatsApp, and we deliver the finished CSV.
What fields are included in an Apollo export?
Each export includes first and last name, verified business email, job title, company name and website, industry, company size, LinkedIn URL and location. The Mobiles package also includes direct dials. Need additional fields? Ask us for a custom export.
Do you offer refunds?
We do not offer refunds on Apollo leads. LinkedIn subscriptions are refunded if a problem occurs with the subscription. See the full refund policy.
How long does delivery take?
Most lead exports, including the Apollo packages, are delivered within 24 hours. Larger or custom requests may take longer, and we confirm the timeline with you on WhatsApp before you pay.
Can I order a different volume or a custom list?
Yes. We specialize in custom B2B data scraping, specific decision-maker targeting and custom Clay workflows. Message us on WhatsApp or LinkedIn with your target requirements and the volume you need, and we will send a quote. Bulk orders get custom discounts.
How does LinkedIn Sales Navigator activation work?
We send an official direct activation link to your LinkedIn email address. No password or personal login access is ever required.
Which countries do your clients come from?
Our clients are mostly sales teams and agencies from the USA, UK, Canada, India and Australia. Lists can be customized by industry, job title, company size and location. Share your target and we will confirm availability.
Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept Wise, PayPal and Payoneer. Standard processing fees (about $1–$2 or more, depending on the provider) are covered by the buyer.
