Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

ValidLeads is a B2B lead generation and data service run by Md Mehedi Hasan. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and by email, and how we use it. You can reach us at validleadsh@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you send us. When you message us on WhatsApp or email us, we receive what you choose to include, such as your name, email address, Apollo search URL, target filters, the number of leads you want, and any notes.

Payments. Payment details are shared with you directly once your order is confirmed. Those providers process your payment details under their own privacy policies. We never see or store your card number or bank login.

Website visits. This website has no user accounts and does not use advertising cookies or analytics tools. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (for example IP address and browser type) under GitHub’s privacy statement. Fonts and icons load from Google Fonts and cdnjs, which can also receive your IP address when the page loads.

3. How we use your information

  • to reply to your questions and prepare quotes and invoices;
  • to deliver your order and provide support;
  • to keep records of orders and payments, including for accounting and legal needs;
  • to protect our service against fraud and misuse.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only where needed to run the service: our email provider (Google Gmail), WhatsApp, the payment provider you choose, and our delivery tools such as shared spreadsheets. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order emails and payment records for as long as needed to support your order, resolve disputes and meet accounting and legal duties. You can ask us to delete other information at any time.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the UK, EU, Canada, Australia, India or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to certain uses, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email validleadsh@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. People who appear in lead data

Our lead lists contain business contact details, such as work emails, job titles and company names, gathered from third-party business data sources and public information. If you appear in a list and want your details removed or corrected, email us with your name and the email address concerned, and we will act on your request and stop including you in future deliveries.

8. Links to other sites

This website links to services we do not control, such as LinkedIn, Facebook and Google Sheets. Their privacy practices are their own, so please read their policies.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email validleadsh@gmail.com. You can also read our Terms & Conditions.
